accuracy=(TP+TN)/(TP+FP+TN+FN)
accuracy=(4+2)/(4+0+1+2)=6/7

recall=TP/(TP+FN)
recall=4/4=1

precision=TP/(TP+FP)
precision=4/5=0.8

F1=2 x precision x recall/(precision+recall)
F1=2 x 0.8 x 1/(0.8+1) = 0.5
